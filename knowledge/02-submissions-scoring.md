# Submissions & scoring

## How submissions work
- Most activities are checklists that need "proof": a screenshot, some text, or a link.
- Some activities have no submission at all — the task is the task, nothing to upload.
- A bare-minimum or one-word "proof" answer is rejected before it's even sent, with a reason shown to the participant. If someone says the app rejected their submission, this is likely why — tell them to add real detail (what they built, a working link, or a real screenshot).

## How grading works
- An AI grader scores each submission 0–100 against a rubric, as a weighted average.
- If a **core requirement** fails (e.g. a broken link), the score is hard-capped at 5.0 regardless of how good the rest is. If a participant says "I did everything but got a low score," ask whether their link/deliverable actually works — that's the most common cause.
- Resubmitting is graded completely fresh. A better resubmission does not average with or build on the earlier score.

## Points and streaks
- Quality bonus = `round(rating/10 × 90) × streak multiplier`, awarded in the same transaction as the grade.
- Points and streaks are an append-only ledger — balances are always summed from history, never edited directly. This means a participant's total can't be "fixed" by editing one row; if something looks wrong, it needs investigation, not a quick manual edit.

## Showcase (Demo Day)
- Business owners pick from graded builds and pay for them live.
- Only passing builds appear in the showcase; the top 30 graded builds are shown, with score displayed as "X/10".
- Ties go to whoever submitted first.

## Leaderboard
- City, state, and national boards, rebuilt every 15 minutes.
- The **city board is the default tab** — a single national board only meaningfully motivates roughly the top 20 out of thousands of participants, so city standing is what most people should look at.
